=========================================
Project : Potentiometer Controlled Servo
=========================================

Components Used
---------------
1. Arduino UNO
2. Potentiometer
3. Servo Motor
4. Jumper Wires

Connections
-----------

Potentiometer

Left Pin -------------> Arduino 5V

Middle Pin -----------> Arduino A0

Right Pin ------------> Arduino GND


Servo Motor

Red Wire -------------> Arduino 5V

Brown/Black Wire -----> Arduino GND

Orange/Yellow Wire ---> Arduino D9


Working
-------

Rotate the potentiometer.

The servo motor rotates according to the knob position.

Expected Output
---------------

Minimum Rotation ---> Servo at 0°

Middle Position ---> Servo at 90°

Maximum Rotation ---> Servo at 180°
