# PIR Motion Alarm

## Aim

Detect motion using a PIR sensor and activate a buzzer.

## Components

- Arduino UNO
- PIR Motion Sensor
- Piezo Buzzer

## Connections

PIR OUT → D2

Buzzer (+) → D8

## Working

Whenever the PIR sensor detects movement, the buzzer turns ON.

Otherwise, it remains OFF.
